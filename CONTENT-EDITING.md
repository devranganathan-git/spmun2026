# Editing SPMUN Website Content

Most changeable conference details live in `config.js`.

Change values such as:
- registrationFee
- paymentUpi
- registrationUrl
- paymentQr
- venue/address
- secretaryGeneral / directorGeneral
- emails / phone / Instagram

For a design or layout change, edit `index.html` and `styles.css`.

Replace `assets/images/payment-qr-placeholder.svg` with the real payment QR (keep the same filename to avoid changing code), or update `config.js` and `index.html` to point to another image.
