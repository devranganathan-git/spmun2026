MEET THE CONFERENCE

SPMUN '26 is the first edition of St. Patrick Model United Nations — an academic simulation where students step into the roles of diplomats, research positions, defend them under scrutiny, build coalitions and work toward outcomes that can survive a vote.

AIPPM SPOTLIGHT

Represent a political position — not a caricature.

AIPPM is a domestic simulation. Delegates represent named individuals and political-party positions, debate with wider parliamentary latitude and work toward consensus statements or joint recommendations. The handbook emphasizes party line, constituency, governing record and knowing where you can concede without abandoning the party position.
